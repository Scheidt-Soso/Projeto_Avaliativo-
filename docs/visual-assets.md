# Assets visuais provisórios

Gerados com a ferramenta integrada ImageGen. Nenhuma foto pessoal foi utilizada. A paisagem é decorativa; a mecânica atual usa os aventureiros do banco original. As imagens foram copiadas integralmente para o projeto, sem alterações no bitmap; as regiões do atlas são exibidas por CSS.

## Paisagem

Arquivo: `public/images/ashen-realm.png`.

Prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide landscape environment background for an original dark fantasy strategy browser game named Coroa de Cinzas. Create a beautifully art-directed hand-painted environment, 16:9 landscape. A forsaken medieval mountain citadel in a valley, seen from a slightly elevated perspective. Very dark crumbling gothic cathedral and tall ruined towers on the right background, distant layered slate mountains and bare pine forests, a tiny red eclipse high in the misty sky, windswept charcoal rocks and ash. Warm amber firelight glows from a few tiny windows against cold desaturated blue-grey atmosphere. Foreground and center are low-detail dark fog and rocky terrain, leaving space for interactive UI and a board to overlay. Strong composition, rich painterly game concept art with etched detail, moody chiaroscuro, melancholy, tactile stone, muted olive and charcoal palette with restrained burnt orange accents. Original world and architecture. Full bleed landscape illustration only, no interface, no text, no logos, no cards, no border, no watermark.

## Atlas das dez classes

Arquivo: `public/images/adventurer-classes.png`. Transparência solicitada e preservada. Primeira linha: Guerreiro, Mago, Arqueiro, Paladino, Ladino. Segunda linha: Clérigo, Bruxo, Druida, Bardo, Monge. Pequenos ajustes de posição horizontal em `config/appearance.php` evitam exibir partes da célula vizinha.

Prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent sprite atlas for an original dark fantasy dungeon browser game. Create exactly TEN distinct high quality full-body pixel-art adventurer sprites arranged on an exact regular grid FIVE COLUMNS by TWO ROWS, each cell exactly same square size, overall canvas aspect ratio 5:2, ideally 2000x800. Each isolated sprite entirely contained and centered in its own cell with equal generous padding, no overlaps, no cropped weapons. Transparent empty space all around each sprite and between cells, no ground plane, no backgrounds. Crisp detailed handcrafted 32-bit pixel art with visible pixel clusters, sinister muted colors, worn armor, tiny ember highlights, front-facing three-quarter idle pose, same scale and art direction across all ten sprites. Order left to right, top row: 1 warrior in battered iron armor and faded red cape with sword and shield; 2 mage in deep blue robes carrying glowing blue staff; 3 archer with grey green hood, longbow and quiver; 4 paladin in aged gold plate armor with white tabard and large sword; 5 rogue with black leather armor and two daggers. Bottom row left to right: 6 cleric in ivory hooded robes with mace and small holy lantern; 7 warlock with tattered purple robes and violet magical staff; 8 druid with green cloak, antler headdress and wooden staff; 9 bard with burgundy cloak and wooden lute; 10 monk with ochre martial wraps, cloth robes and glowing fists. Original character designs, no famous characters. No text, no labels, no letters, no cell borders, no logos, no interface. Pixel art sprites only.
